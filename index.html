Graffitti Disguised As Poem

My “author” saw the internet and thought
that what these one point four nine billion sites
require is one more page and then he fought
a placid battle In various states of dress
and here I am. So was I worth his time?
and more importantly am I worth yours?
You could be hearing a Chinese cymbal chime
flying through a video forest’s endless floors
or learning how to save a life. You’re here.
How can I make this moment worth your time?
No that’s an unctuous and subservient thought
A poet should think but never deign to write.
This is a fine between-place for a note.
Perhaps I should have written you a joke…
